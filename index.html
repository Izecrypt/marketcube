Marketcube works with businesses that sell big-ticket things: real estate, automobiles, established services. The problem

Ad platforms can only optimize for what they can count. If your campaigns target form fills, the algorithm learns to find form fillers. Your real buyers behave differently, and the platform never learns it.

The gap between a browser and a buyer is where your ad budget quietly goes.

The practice

We instrument your funnel end to end, including the WhatsApp conversations where your deals actually happen. We score every lead on ability to afford and quality of engagement, validate that score against your closed sales, and hand it back to the ad platforms as the target.

Then we measure incrementally, so you know what the spend added, not what the dashboard claims.

01Instrumentevery quote view, call, message, and financing signal captured, with your consent and ownership
02Scoreone validated lead score per business, built from its own sales history, not a template
03Retargetthe score becomes the ad platforms' conversion event; your ads start hunting buyers
04Proveincrementality measurement from day one; the report includes what did not work
