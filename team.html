Delivery pipeline

Six stages per client.

Open a stage for its output.

1. Intake Week 0

Funnel audit, tracking plan, data access. Output: instrumentation spec.

2. Score build Weeks 1 to 3

Capture pipeline (site events, WhatsApp, call and email transcripts, financing signals), intent analysis, correlation with sales history, validation. Output: lead score v1.

3. Target swap Week 3

The score becomes the ad platform’s conversion event. Thresholds tuned for about 50 qualified events a week. Output: live loop.

4. Growth layer Ongoing

Curriculum modules that fit the client, creative pipelines, programmatic SEO, behavioural email.

5. Measure Monthly

Incrementality check (geo holdouts or MMM-lite). Honest report, including negatives. Every client figure must be reproducible from raw events.

6. Sales layer Ongoing

Belfort funnel training, A/B/C bucket discipline, call review sampling.

Hard boundaries

Five rules, no exceptions.

Never

  • Publish client data, ledgers or names without written consent and owner approval
  • Spend or commit client money
  • Sign contracts, send priced proposals or make job offers
  • Promise results

Always

  • Promise measurement and honest reporting
  • Verify any public claim about a competitor
  • Route contracts and pricing changes to a Chief of Strategy verdict first
  • Keep books private; claims verifiable
